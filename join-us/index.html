Join the Miller Lab

The Miller Lab is a collaborative, interdisciplinary research group bridging the Donald Danforth Plant Science Center and Saint Louis University. We work across evolutionary biology, root architecture, perennial crop development, bioinformatics, and genomics.

We are always eager to connect with curious, enthusiastic researchers at all career stages who are excited about perennial plants and agricultural sustainability.

Postdoctoral Researchers

We welcome inquiries from prospective postdoctoral scholars whose research interests align with perennial plant evolution, grapevine rootstocks, root phenomics, or breeding genetics.

Allison is glad to work with potential postdocs to co-develop new research initiatives and support applications for independent fellowships (such as the NSF Postdoctoral Research Fellowship in Biology, USDA NIFA fellowships, and institutional programs).

Students

Undergraduate & Summer Researchers

Undergraduate students from Saint Louis University can gain hands-on laboratory, greenhouse, and field experience for academic credit or through work-study positions.

We also regularly mentor summer students through the Danforth Center's NSF-funded Research Experiences for Undergraduates (REU) program and regional student internship initiatives.

How to Reach Out

If you are interested in joining the lab, please send an email to Allison Miller (amiller@danforthcenter.org or allison.j.miller@slu.edu) with:

  • Your current CV or résumé.
  • A brief summary (1–2 paragraphs) describing your research background, academic path, and scientific interests.
  • A short note on why you are drawn to the Miller Lab's research in perennial plant biology, root systems, or crop evolution.
  • For graduate applicants: mention whether you hold or plan to apply for external fellowships (e.g., NSF GRFP).
