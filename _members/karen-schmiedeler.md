*Pronouns: she/her*

Karen joined the lab in 2023 as a SLU undergraduate researcher working for the New Roots for Restoration Biology Integration Institute.  After graduating in 2024 with her degree in biology, Karen continued in the lab as a technician.  She first became interested in research through working as an undergraduate fellow at the Tyson Research Center, where she helped study tick and wildlife ecology.  She then developed an interest in plants through working at the Litzsinger Road Ecology Center, where she worked on restoration and prairie reconstruction.  She is always excited to learn more about plants and the natural world.  In her free time, Karen enjoys hiking, board games, cooking, and playing the guitar.
