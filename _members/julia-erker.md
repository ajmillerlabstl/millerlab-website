Julia is an undergraduate at Saint Louis University, in her junior year, majoring in Environmental Science and minoring in English. She has been a lover of nature since learning that plants go through cellular respiration in third grade and became passionate about protecting our natural world when she visited Glacier National Park when she was 14 and got to experience all of the park's wonder. This is also where she developed a love of hiking, one of her favorite things to do, along with reading, writing, discovering great new coffee spots, sketching, and taking care of her house plants. Through my undergraduate studies she has developed specific interests in plant ecology and soil sciences and hopes to explore these more in depth during her time with the Miller Lab.
