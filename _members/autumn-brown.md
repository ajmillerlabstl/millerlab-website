Autumn Brown is from a small town in the Ozarks. She received her Bachelors at the University of Arkansas and her Masters at the University of Wisconsin. She has worked with a wide variety of crops before coming to Danforth including Muscadines, Grapes, Blackberries, Raspberries, Cannabis and Quinoa. Her interests are in specialty crops, breeding/genetics and ethnobotany. She enjoys hanging out with her family, friends and cat (Stevie), crafting, gardening, reading and learning new things.
