*Pronouns: she/her*

Gillian grew up in the Chicago area and graduated from Kenyon College in 2025 with a BA in Molecular Biology and a minor in French. There, she studied the genetic mechanism of seasonal reproduction in the model moss *Physcomitrium patens,*and developed an admiration for plant form and a passion for leveraging molecular tools to learn more about what goes on inside cells. Gillian is excited to use her time in the Miller Lab to learn more about the ways in which plants persist in their environments and to admire the vast diversity of form that exists in the perennials she works with. In her free time, she enjoys baking sweet treats, testing out new chocolate chip cookie recipes, making linoleum prints, reading, and running.
