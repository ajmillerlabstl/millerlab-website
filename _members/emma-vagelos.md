*Pronouns: she/her*

Emma grew up in the Bay Area California but came to St. Louis for an undergraduate degree at WashU in Environmental analysis with minors in Earth Science and Education Studies. She first became interested in agriculture in high school, but spent her college summers working on different types of farms. Her interest in perennials came from her first summer working on an organic  Lowbush blueberry farm. In course work, she focused on research methods and field skills, developing an  interest in how people interact with and perceive their environment. She spent the past year teaching biology and ecology, but is excited to get back into research to dig into the biology of the human-modified ecosystem. In her free time she enjoys cooking, reading, and finding new spots to swim outside.
