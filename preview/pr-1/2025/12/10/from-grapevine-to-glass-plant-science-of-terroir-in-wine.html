From Grapevine to Glass: Plant Science of Terroir in Wine

Allison Miller Lab Principal Investigator (2006 - present)
December 10, 2025 October 20, 2026
news vitis

How does a grape’s environment affect its taste? A recent feature by the Donald Danforth Plant Science Center highlights the Miller Lab’s collaborative work investigating the biological mechanisms of terroir.

By studying grafted grapevines across diverse climates, soils, and rootstock-scion combinations, our research examines how root systems interact with the soil microbiome and climate to shape vine morphology, physiology, and grape quality.
