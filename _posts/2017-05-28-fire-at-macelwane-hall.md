On Thursday May 25, 2017 there was a fire at Macelwane Hall, our home at Saint Louis University ([SL Post Dispatch coverage](http://www.stltoday.com/news/local/crime-and-courts/no-injuries-after-fire-breaks-out-at-three-story-slu/article_bb32f6ee-f3aa-56a9-a96f-e52098100706.html)). We are very grateful that no one was hurt. The building sustained significant fire, smoke, and water damage. All offices and labs occupied by our group were impacted. Thanks to the St. Louis Fire Department, Saint Louis University leadership, and our friends and colleagues for support and kind words during this difficult time.
