Thank you to theSaint Louis University Presidential Research Fund**for supporting our project entitled "Diversity and diversification in North American grapevines (*V. riparia* and *V. rupestris*)." We are grateful for this award!!  The proposed work will support part of Laura Klein's dissertation investigating the impacts of interspecific hybridization on leaf morphology and population genetic structure in North American grapevines.
