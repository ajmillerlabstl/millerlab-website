The Foundation for Food & Agriculture Research (FFAR) has awarded funding to Danforth Center plant scientists, including Allison Miller, to accelerate breeding and domestication of perennial crops that protect soil health and water quality.

This multi-institutional project leverages high-dimensional phenomics and genomic tools to assess trait covariation in emerging perennial candidates—such as intermediate wheatgrass (*Thinopyrum intermedium*), sainfoin (*Onobrychis viciifolia*), and silphium (*Silphium integrifolium*).

Learn more about the award and research objectives at the [Danforth Center Announcement](https://www.danforthcenter.org/news/ffar-taps-danforth-center-plant-scientists-for-crop-research-to-preserve-soil-and-water-health/).
