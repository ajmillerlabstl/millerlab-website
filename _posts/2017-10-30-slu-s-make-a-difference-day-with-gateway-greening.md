Each year, Saint Louis University hosts “Make A Difference Day,” where approximately 4,000 SLU students, faculty, and staff volunteer with community programs across the St. Louis region. “Make a Difference Day,” MADD for short, has garnered the title of the largest collegiate volunteer effort in the Midwest and the largest day of service in Missouri. In addition to helping coordinate and connect SLU volunteer groups with partner organizations, the university provides lunches, water, gloves, and MADD t-shirts to everyone who participates (**pictured below**). Thanks, SLU!
The Miller Lab & Co. were excited to get involved with MADD, this year taking place on Saturday, October 28th. We partnered with [Gateway Greening](http://www.gatewaygreening.org), a non-profit organization that operates a network of community and youth gardens in St. Louis and hosts educational programs about sustainable urban agriculture, among other activities. We were placed at the [McPherson Community Garden](http://www.gatewaygreening.org/gardens/community-resource-garden-directory/), a plot in the Skinker-DeBaliviere neighborhood with over 60 raised beds that can be rented out by community members. The garden is celebrating its 20th anniversary in early November.

*The McPherson Community Garden, located at 5943 McPherson Ave., St. Louis, MO 63112.*

![]({{ 'images/blog/img-3319.jpg' | relative_url }}) Andy Cross, a local artist and the McPherson Garden coordinator, lead us through our project for the day. While many raised beds were in good shape, featuring produce like cherry tomatoes, raspberries, and brussel sprouts, there were several that needed weeding and soil turnover to prep them for planting in the spring. The thirteen of us restored eleven raised beds over the course of the morning, fortifying a handful of them with a compost mixture of leaves. We were also able to pull weeds in between beds and around the plot itself, while two Miller Lab helpers constructed a fairy-garden (**see gallery below**). We ended the day in Andy’s art studio down the street, enjoying donuts and lunch together and warming up inside.

We thoroughly enjoyed the opportunity to volunteer with Gateway Greening, getting our hands in the dirt, and seeing the success of St. Louis community gardening first hand. We’re looking forward to setting up another volunteer day in the spring. Many thanks to Andy, Gateway Greening, and the MADD committee for orchestrating our group activity, providing tools, and free lunch!
