Alex Linan has advanced to candidacy! His dissertation research is focused on the diversification of the ebony and persimmon genus (*Diospyros*) in Madagascar and the Mascarenes.  Alex is co-advised by Christy Edwards, Conservation Geneticist at the Missouri Botanical Garden. Congratulations, Alex!
