Can perennial grains build a more climate-resilient food system? St. Louis Public Radio covered the emerging regional ecosystem around Kernza, highlighting local initiatives such as Blue Jay Brewing's Kernza beer and research partnerships between Saint Louis University, the Donald Danforth Plant Science Center, and The Land Institute.

Unlike annual crops that require replanting every year, perennial grains develop extensive, multi-year root networks that sequester carbon, stabilize topsoil, and reduce agricultural runoff.
