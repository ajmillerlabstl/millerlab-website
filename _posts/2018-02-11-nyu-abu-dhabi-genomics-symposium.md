Posted by Allison:

In February 2018 I visited [New York University Abu Dhabi](https://nyuad.nyu.edu/en/), located on Saadiyat Island in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates, for the NYU AD Center for Genomics and Systems Biology annual conference on Genome Systems. [2018 Genome Systems VIII](https://nyuad.nyu.edu/en/events/2018/february/center-for-genomics-&-systems-biology-viii.html) included a wide diversity of talks highlighting recent advances in genomics and systems biology. I presented some of our work on *Vitis* diversification and root system impacts on shoot system phenotypes.  In addition to hearing lots of exciting science, hosts Michael Purugganan, Jonathan  Flowers, Muriel Gros-Balthazard, Khaled Hazzouri showed us Abu Dhabi and Dubai. What an incredible part of the world! Many thanks to NYU AD and our gracious hosts for wonderful visit.
