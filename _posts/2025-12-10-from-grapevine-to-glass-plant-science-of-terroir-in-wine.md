How does a grape’s environment affect its taste? A recent feature by the [Donald Danforth Plant Science Center](https://www.danforthcenter.org/news/from-grapevine-to-glass-the-plant-science-of-terroir-in-wine/) highlights the Miller Lab’s collaborative work investigating the biological mechanisms of *terroir*.

By studying grafted grapevines across diverse climates, soils, and rootstock-scion combinations, our research examines how root systems interact with the soil microbiome and climate to shape vine morphology, physiology, and grape quality.

Read the full story at the [Danforth Plant Science Center News](https://www.danforthcenter.org/news/from-grapevine-to-glass-the-plant-science-of-terroir-in-wine/).
