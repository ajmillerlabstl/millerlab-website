Allison and Laura attended the [International Botanical Congress](http://www.ibc2017.cn/) in Shenzhen China July 23-29. More than 7,000 botanists from around the world attended the event, which happens every six years. A fishing village that began transforming just 40 years ago, today Shenzhen is a city of more than 15 million people with a strong focus on biology and genomics. Shenzhen is home to the Fairy Lake Botanical Garden, BGI genome sequencing center, and the China National Genebank.
