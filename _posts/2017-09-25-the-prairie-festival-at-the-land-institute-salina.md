Several members of the Miller Lab attended the 2017 [Prairie Festival at The Land Institute](https://landinstitute.org/news-events/prairie-festival/). The festival's theme was"Urban Agriculture and Rural Agrarianism: Toward a Perennial Future." Two days of outstanding presentation on a range of topics related to perennial agriculture were terrific. We enjoyed tours of the breeding plots as well as a wonderful walk through the surrounding native prairie. Thanks to The Land Institute for another awesome fall festival!
