The Prairie Festival at The Land Institute, Salina, KS

Allison Miller Lab Principal Investigator (2006 - present)
September 25, 2017 October 19, 2026
lab blog

Several members of the Miller Lab attended the 2017 Prairie Festival at The Land Institute. The festival’s theme was”Urban Agriculture and Rural Agrarianism: Toward a Perennial Future.” Two days of outstanding presentation on a range of topics related to perennial agriculture were terrific. We enjoyed tours of the breeding plots as well as a wonderful walk through the surrounding native prairie. Thanks to The Land Institute for another awesome fall festival!
