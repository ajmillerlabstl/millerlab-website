About the lab

The Miller Lab studies perennial plant biology, diversity, and evolution. Members work across bioinformatics, ethnobotany, evolutionary ecology, field botany, genetics, genomics, morphology, root biology, and statistics.

The lab is a joint faculty partnership between two St. Louis institutions, and collaborates with scientists at botanical gardens, universities, research centers, nonprofits, and governmental organizations across the country and world.
